Privacy Policy – OMAGIC Chess

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the OMAGIC chess app for Android (the "App") handles information. The App is published by vibetop ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at vibetop.vn@gmail.com.

In short
  • You can play without creating an account.
  • We do not run servers, and we do not collect, sell or share personal information ourselves.
  • Your settings, guest name, the game in progress and your puzzle progress are stored only on your device. They are not included in Android backups or in transfers to a new phone.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK collects some device data, described below.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in the App.

1. Information stored only on your device

The App stores the following on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else:

InformationWhat it containsWhyHow long it is kept
SettingsSound, vibration, board display options (legal moves, last move, coordinates, king in check, opening name) and "Always Promote to Queen".To remember your preferences.Until you change them or delete the App's data.
LanguageEnglish or Vietnamese.To show the App in your language.Until you change it or delete the App's data.
Guest profileWhether you chose "Continue as Guest", and a guest name generated at random (for example "Guest #1234").To show your profile in the App.Until you log out or delete the App's data.

Your choice in Google's consent form (section 3) is also stored on your device, by Google's consent software, so that it is applied each time ads are requested.

The chess computer (the Arasan engine) runs entirely on your device. Your games and moves are not uploaded anywhere.

No backups or transfers. The App opts out of Android backup: the information above is not copied to your Google Drive backup and is not moved to a new phone when you transfer your data. If you change or reset your phone, the App starts from scratch.

Deleting it. You can delete all of this information at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings (for example Settings → Apps → the App → Storage → Clear storage; the exact path depends on your device) or by uninstalling the App. "Log Out" in the App's settings removes the guest name.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App automatically collects the following data from your device and sends it to Google:

DataDetails (as described by Google)
IP addressMay be used to estimate the general location of your device.
Product interactionsApp launches, taps and video views.
Diagnostic informationPerformance of the App and the SDK, such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage.
Device identifiersAndroid advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies: the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We only receive aggregated reports from AdMob (for example, the number of ads shown), which do not identify you.

3. Consent for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in one of these regions, the App shows Google's consent form before ads are requested, so you can choose whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options inside the App. If you do not give consent, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.

4. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Android advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact path depends on your device).
  • Turn sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings.
  • Delete everything stored by the App by clearing its data or uninstalling it.

5. Permissions

  • Internet access – used to load ads.
  • Vibration – a short vibration when a move is made; you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID – used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in section 2.

6. Analytics and crash reporting

The App currently does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. If we add one, we will update this policy before it takes effect.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

8. Data retention and your rights

We do not keep any personal information on our own systems. Information on your device is kept for the times listed in section 1 and is never sent to us. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained by Google according to its policies; you can manage it through your Google and device settings. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Because we do not hold such data, requests about advertising data are handled by Google, but you are always welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

9. International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries other than your own, under the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the App's store listing or inside the App.
